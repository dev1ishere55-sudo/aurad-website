How to get $AURAD at launch

  1. Get a Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare. Write your seed phrase down offline and never share it with anyone.
  2. Add a little SOL from an exchange you already trust. Only use money you can afford to lose completely.
  3. Wait for the official contract address. It is posted only at launch (30 Sept, 13:00 UTC) on our official X account @auradebttm and this website. Anything earlier is fake.
  4. Verify before you touch it (see below).
  5. Open the token on Pump.fun using the verified address, connect your wallet and swap.

Verify the contract on Solscan

  • Paste the address into solscan.io. The token name should be Aura Debt and the symbol AURAD.
  • Check that it matches the address on both @auradebttm on X and this site, character by character.
  • Check the creation time lines up with launch, not days earlier.
  • Look at the holder list. Be careful if a few wallets hold most of the supply.
  • Copycat tokens with the same name will appear. The address is the only thing that counts.
